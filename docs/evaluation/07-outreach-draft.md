## Questions for a potential Army sponsor

**Official data use**
1. Would any planned use make AFT results entered in the app an official record, or subject to records rules?
2. Can Soldiers use the app for personal practice data only, and what notice should they see?
3. Is unit or leader visibility of scores ever appropriate in a tool like this, and under what controls?

**Hosting**
4. Our current hosting (Vercel, commercial) has not been assessed for official data. Which hosting would be
   required for any official use, and at what impact level?
5. Are there restrictions on accessing the site from government networks or devices?

**Privacy**
6. Would a Privacy Threshold Analysis, Privacy Impact Assessment or SORN be needed for a pilot or later use?
7. What data minimization or retention rules apply to fitness scores and dates of birth?

**Cyber authorization**
8. What authorization (for example RMF/ATO, or a lighter path for a pilot with no government data) would apply,
   and who would be the authorizing official?
9. What security testing would you require, and would the government perform or accept it?

**Acquisition and endorsement**
10. What acquisition route, if any, would apply (for example a pilot agreement, SBIR, or a software
    acquisition pathway)?
11. What may we say publicly about any review or pilot, and what wording must we avoid to prevent implying
    endorsement?
12. Who should review training content (H2F roles, clinicians), and how should their approval be recorded?
