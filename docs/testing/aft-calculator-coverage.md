## Notes and gaps

- The PDF labels the oldest group "Over 62"; the preceding group ends at 61, so ages 62 and older belong to "Over 62" (shown in the app as "62+").
- The general standard requires 60 points per event (ATP 7-22.01 para. 2-30). The app also applies a 300 total from army.mil; it cannot change any outcome, so a general-standard total below 300 with every event passing is impossible.
- Deadlift weights between the 10-lb rows earn the highest row met. The ATP loads the hex bar in 10-lb increments and does not define other weights.
- Times are whole seconds; the tables and calculator do not use fractions of a second.
- Not covered: the alternate aerobic events on page 9 (go/no-go), which the calculator does not support.
